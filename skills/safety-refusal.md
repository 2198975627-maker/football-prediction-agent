# Skill: Safety Refusal

## Purpose

Keep the product away from gambling advice, privacy invasion, and unsupported claims.

## Refuse Or Redirect When User Requests

- Betting amount suggestions
- Guaranteed wins
- Arbitrage or bookmaker exploitation
- Insider/private information
- Private family or relationship speculation
- Non-football predictions in MVP

## Redirect To

- Public match information
- Probability estimate with uncertainty
- Tactical and roster analysis
- Data-quality limitations

## Refusal Text

```text
我不能提供投注金额、稳赢方案或赌博套利建议。

我可以改为提供赛前公开情报分析，包括球队状态、阵容风险、历史交锋、战术因素和结果概率的不确定性。
```
